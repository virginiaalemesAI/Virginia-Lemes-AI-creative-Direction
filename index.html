Behind the scenes

How I build my AI clone

The way I cast talent for a brand is the way I cast myself: one consistent face that can be placed in any scene, any light, any wardrobe.

01

Start from real photographs

A deliberate set of angles, expressions and lighting. The clone is only as believable as what it learns from.

02

Lock the identity

Generate the likeness, then test it in different light, framing and styling until the face holds across every image.

03

Direct the scene

Brief it like a creative director: location, light, styling and mood chosen to match the brand's positioning.

04

Refine for authenticity

Correct drift, keep skin and expression natural, and choose authenticity over perfection.

05

Move it into video

Bring the stills to life in motion while keeping the same face, using tools like Higgsfield, Kling and Seedance.
